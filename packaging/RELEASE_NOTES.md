**New in 0.2.1 — working with sections:**
- **Copy, Cut, and Paste** (⌘C, ⌘X, ⌘V). Pasted tiles land under the mouse pointer, already selected so you can drag or turn them. Sections can be pasted into another map.
- With several tiles selected, **Turn, Mirror, and Flip work on the whole section as one block**, so the tiles stay joined up instead of each turning in its own spot.

**New in 0.2.0 — background image:** put a photo or plan under (or over) your map to trace a real place at true scale. Open it with **View ▸ Choose Background Image…** or the **Background Image** panel on the right.
- **Set Scale from a Known Distance…** — click both ends of something you know the length of and type it in feet; the image is resized so 5 ft = 1 square. Or type the **Image width** directly.
- **Move Image** — drag it into line, then click **Done Moving**.
- **Opacity** slider, and **Over tiles / Under tiles** (press **B** to flip; ⇧B hides it).
- It pans and zooms with the tiles, is saved with the map, and is **never printed or exported**.

**0.1.5:** pinch, mouse-wheel, and toolbar zooming.

**Which file do I need?** Download the file ending in **arm64.dmg**. It's for Macs with Apple chips (M1/M2/M3/M4…); older Intel Macs are no longer supported.

**Installing:** open the DMG and drag Tile Mapper to Applications. The first time, macOS will block it because it isn't registered with Apple: open **System Settings ▸ Privacy & Security**, scroll down, and click **Open Anyway** next to Tile Mapper. After that it opens normally. Full steps are in READ_ME_FIRST.txt inside the DMG.

**Printing to scale:** File ▸ Print / Export… ▸ *Full size — split into printer pages*, then print the PDF at **Scale 100%** (not "Fit to page"). Measure the 1-inch square on any page to check.
